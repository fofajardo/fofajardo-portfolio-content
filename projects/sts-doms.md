Our proposed solution involves the development of an electronic logging system aimed at reducing paper and pen usage, leading to environmental benefits, improving the efficiency of the logging process, and providing UPLB students with a more convenient and streamlined system.

The envisioned electronic logging system relies on the application of scientific and technological principles, especially in the fields of computer science and software engineering. With this expertise, we intend to create a user-friendly and highly efficient logging system.
